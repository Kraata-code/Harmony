Version 1.1.0

=== New features ===
• Recognize nearby music with the microphone, regional recognition databases, playback, and recognition history.
• Identify local files and update their metadata and cover art using acoustic fingerprints, AcoustID, and MusicBrainz.
• Edit the title, artist, and cover art of local songs manually.
• Update metadata for complete folders in the background with progress and result summaries.
• Check for, download, and install updates from GitHub releases inside the app.

=== Improvements ===
• Improved YouTube playback fallback and thumbnail resolution.
• Improved local metadata matching, the floating mini player, and local AI performance.

Note: Folder metadata updates can overwrite existing metadata and cover art.
